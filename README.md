# A-Practical-Starter-Guide
Studying full time and working part time is hard, but it is very doable with a plan. Start with your timetable.
This can be very difficult to manage, but if managed well can work well. Consider how many hours you can fit in according to your timetable. Include specific times for lectures, exams and a realistic estimation of hours. Consider how much energy you will have.

Consider the type of work you would like to do. If tutoring, helping in libraries etc. it can easily fit with lectures. If working in retail or hospitality, you may be able to be very flexible with hours, but may struggle to balance work and study if working late. Data entry and checking in online works well in the evenings however does not pay as well.

If you are in 2 nd year and want to earn as much money as possible, you may be overloading yourself. However, limiting yourself to just 2 set shifts a week (and no extra shifts in exam weeks), can help this.

Be careful of scams. If an employer is genuine they will not ask you to pay any money upfront. Be wary of any offers for work without interviews. If an offer seems to pay very highly for little hours of work, make sure to double check it.

Manage your workload. Check how many hours you are doing and your grades. If grades are dropping, change your schedule. Prioritise studying, and money making should support this.
